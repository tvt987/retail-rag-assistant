# Returns policy (fictional demo)

Customers may return an unopened item within 14 days of delivery with a receipt. The item must retain its original packaging and accessories.

Opened hygiene products cannot be returned unless they were defective on arrival. A defective product may be reported within 30 days of delivery with an order number and photos of the issue.

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method. Processing normally takes 5 to 10 business days after inspection.
